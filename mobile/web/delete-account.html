Delete your Dobha Dobha account

You can delete your account yourself at any time, in the app or on the web.

How to delete it

  1. Open the Dobha Dobha app, or go to this website and log in.
  2. Tap Me, then the gear icon for Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account, read what happens, and tap Continue.
  4. Enter your password (or type DELETE if you sign in with Google) and tap Delete my account.
Before you can delete your account, finish any orders that are still in progress and withdraw any money in your Dobha wallet.

What is deleted

Straight away: your name, username, email address, phone number, profile picture, bio and area, shop details, listings that never sold (with their photos and videos), likes, saves, comments, cart, offers in progress, follows, notifications and sign-in sessions.

What is kept

Records of orders and payments you were part of are kept, with your profile removed, for as long as South African law requires (usually five years), so the other person in each order still has a record of it. Chat messages and reviews you sent stay with the people you sent them to, shown as from a deleted account.

Can't log in?

Use Forgot password on the log in screen, or Need help? then Contact support in the app, pick My account, and ask us to delete your account. Use the email address on your account so we can confirm it is yours.
